# DELTA BRIEF — Sessions: Per-Day Step Line

Supersedes: `DELTA-sessions-beads.md` in full (bead system removed) and the transitional bead+trend hybrid. A cumulative-staircase variant was built and REJECTED — it changed the y-axis meaning (running total) and read as broken next to per-day siblings. Recorded here so it isn't re-proposed.

## Form Δ
- Sessions per day as a step line: flat integer plateau per day at that day's count, sharp vertical steps between days, rest days on the baseline. Angular where the ridge is smooth — integer data gets integer geometry; that is the card's uniqueness inside the line family.
- Same anatomy as ridge: charcoal 1.5 line (round joins), sage fill beneath (data-cast light), dashed AVG rule at true height, draw-in entrance, 0–6 fixed y-scale (stable across 7/14 ranges).

## Marks & interaction Δ
- Doctrine applies: no ordinary markers; hollow at peak day; breathing solid dot riding the line end at today's level; selection solid + plumb.
- Pin verb (shared with ridge): tap a day → plumb hairline + point label "WED 20 / 6 sessions"; hover previews; keyboard arrows walk days. Hit zones are full-height day slots.
- Captions: default "+N vs prior week"; selected "+X % vs daily average".

- Beads, droplet/plip physics, resting bead, aura columns, measured trend overlay, and their storyboards. None survive to the Uno build.
